Can I see the space before I pay?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to book a tour at a time that suits you. Since we’re open 24/7, that includes evenings.

What do I get for PKR 15,000 a month?

A hot desk in the shared workspace with 24/7 access, fast internet, generator backup, air conditioning, parking and use of the prayer area.

What is a hot desk?

You sit at any free desk in the shared workspace, instead of a desk reserved only for you. It’s the most affordable way to work here.

Is it really open 24/7?

Yes. You can come in and work at any hour, any day of the week. That makes it easy to work your clients’ hours in the US, UK or Gulf.

What happens during load-shedding?

Our backup generator keeps the power on, so your calls, uploads and work carry on.

How does pricing work for teams?

Team pricing depends on how many people you have and when you want to start. Fill in the quote form and we’ll reply on WhatsApp.
